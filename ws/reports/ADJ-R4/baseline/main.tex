\title{The spectral gap of the spin-2 AKLT model on the square lattice}

\begin{abstract}
We prove that the spin-2 AKLT Hamiltonian on the $L \times N$ square-lattice torus has a unique ground state and a spectral gap bounded below uniformly in all even $L, N$ larger than a fixed constant. The proof is computer-assisted. A finite family of local inequalities is verified by computer in exact integer and rational arithmetic, and two bounds on weighted sums over lattice trails in floating-point arithmetic with upward rounding. The proof replaces each spin by a finite tight frame of coherent states, which turns the quantum problem into the spectral gap of a heat-bath dynamics for a finite-state Markov field. A finite-size criterion and the theory of Martinelli, Olivieri and Schonmann reduce this gap to a coupling estimate for window marginals in large squares. We prove that estimate with an exact factorization over a separating cut, which converts it into bounds on pair correlations next to removed sites, and these bounds are obtained from a walk expansion and a linear recursion whose kernel is controlled by computer-assisted estimates.
\end{abstract}
