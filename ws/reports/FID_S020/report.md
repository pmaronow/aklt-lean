Omitting injectivity is faithful to a weighted law. Aggregation preserves the functional on every function, not merely on polynomials. Conversely, every finite positive weighted unit-vector law can be enumerated without repetitions. Thus repeated labels introduce no additional mathematical laws or unintended degeneracy.
