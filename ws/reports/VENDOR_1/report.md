## Open proof work and precise limits

No vendoring issue remains open. The following are manuscript/certificate obligations, not
missing source copies or claims that a donor supplies an unproved theorem:

- Finite conditioning must be connected to the actual positive finite-product L2 operators.
  The complete maximal-coupling/overlap/completion result, pair-dependent surgery, product
  density comparison, labelled block-to-site comparison, and Hamming-to-gap argument remain
  new finite lemmas. TV contraction alone proves none of those whole statements.
- The weighted reversible inner product needs its finite-coordinate self-adjoint realization.
  QICLean's spectral theorem concerns an actual Hermitian matrix; no stationarity or symmetry
  of a campaign chain is silently assumed.
- Physlib's algebraic tensor modules need the chosen polynomial and Hermitian realization for
  spin 2, determinant divisibility and the torus kernel. Fixed monomial norms, the three
  rotated 12-letter frames and the finite tight-frame identity are still exact calculations.
  An equal-dimensional isometry theorem is not substituted for a rectangular tight frame.
- Balaban's nearest-neighbour connectivity assumptions and boundary convention differ from
  the plan's star-connected full sup-distance boundary. The triangular/mod-two adaptation,
  nonemptiness, cross thickening and weighted distance/count estimate need proofs. Neither
  Balaban nor Potts was mislabelled as proving the plan's stronger statement verbatim.
- Dyadic primitives do not supply automata, Gram/residual soundness, integer reconstruction,
  Bellman soundness, witness files, or large kernel replay. Fixed-denominator integer inner
  loops should use the campaign's structural checkers; `toRat` is the semantic interface,
  not a mandate to run rational arithmetic inside the large certificates.
