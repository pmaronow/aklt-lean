## Proof

The actual twelve-atom formulas give the cubic local normalizer and first moment. A sum-of-squares identity proves the `3/5` local mean bound; extra nonnegative terms `(1 - normSq a) * (5 + dot b c)^2` and its cyclic counterparts handle fields in the closed unit ball directly. The D law follows from its explicit mixture with the zero atom.

The proof works directly with the active-site subtype of `Network`, retaining the original-leg product exactly. It does not construct a separate `FiniteSpinNetwork` indexed by `Fin n`. The geometry lemmas show that the three neighbours have no mutual bonds or original legs and lose their bond to the departure site. Factor collection therefore gives three independent cubic local factors on each outside-spin fibre.

All fibres are summed explicitly using uniform coordinate resampling. Summing replacements at the three sites multiplies the original full sum by `13^3`, a strictly positive integer that can be cancelled. Zero coefficient fibres contribute zero, so no fibre-mass division is needed. The local norm bound gives the pinned response lower bound `41/50`. The proof also handles a zero total normalizer explicitly, where the defined expectations vanish.

For the free model, every coordinate monomial has nonnegative site integral, using the alphabet's coordinate sign reflections. Natural-coefficient polynomial expansion then proves nonnegativity of every free pair numerator and of the normalizer. Thus every free pair expectation is nonnegative and the free response scalar is at least one.

Finally, the exponential integral representation proves logarithmic-mean monotonicity. Mathlib's four-term atanh estimate at `9/91` proves the paper's crude enclosure at `41/50`. Exact rational arithmetic gives

```text

```
