       Abstract. The evenness assumption on both torus periods can be removed. Three explicit, dis-
       joint, antipodal spherical designs give positive classical weights on every torus. The sign change is
       made only after lifting a conditional box to the plane. The existing continuous estimate then gives
       a uniform window bound for the resulting inhomogeneous finite-state specifications. A finite-state
       box-gap argument requires no translation invariance. Uniqueness holds for every pair of periods at
       least three; more generally the ground space on every finite simple graph of maximum degree four
       is computed exactly.
