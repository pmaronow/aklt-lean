## Selected H1 replacement: 38 universal trees

The exact family is [cores_universal_final.json](data/cores_universal_final.json).
A component is a path; each vertex records its U/D law, whether it is a
passage neighbour, whether it is the outgoing neighbour, and its external
field radii. A vertex labelled m is an already integrated mean vector in the
ball of its listed radius. Consecutive vertices are joined by 1+n.n'.
Different components have independent variables. There are at most three
passage neighbours and at most one outgoing mark; the outgoing mark can be
absent after a zero-spin term in a convex decomposition. Unit directions of
all listed fields are arbitrary. Zero fields can be inserted by affinity.
This interpretation, not the archived pattern IDs in the file, defines a model.

For a model F, let Z be its base partition function and Cij its unnormalized
inserted pair moments at the marked passage neighbours. Define

`Zcur = Z + (1/3) sum_{i<j} Cij`,

`Znext = Z + (1/5) Cfg` if there are exactly two non-outgoing neighbours,
and `Znext=Z` otherwise. These are precisely the passage moment identities.
For a deterministic mean vertex, insertion of n means insertion of that
vector. The source code's field 3R at such a vertex is an algebraic encoding
of its degree-one tensor; it is not a physical field assumption.

The numerical decoder uses the paper's ring order E,C0,N,C1,W,C2,S,C3,
omits zero-radius fields, and gives each remaining field one variable index.
A collapsed mean has one such index. Two unit slots at a U corner form the
same unnormalized symmetric-pair basis as Appendix D; other slots remain
single qubits. This fixes inversion indices and the central-sector integer
basis. The JSON path coordinates fix this encoding; archived pattern IDs
are only provenance labels.

The new finite input is **H1-tree**: for every model in this list and every
unit field direction, `Zcur >= c_type Znext`, at the unchanged two constants.
It is certified by 21 small mean bounds (plus two unit-pair formulas), 22 scalar quadratic tests and 16
reconstructed Pauli-sector matrices. The old conclusion on all artificially
filled D-corner slots is neither needed nor asserted.

The arithmetic witnesses are [universal_certificates.jsonl](data/universal_certificates.jsonl),
[universal_mean_checks.json](data/universal_mean_checks.json), and
[universal_exact_summary.json](data/universal_exact_summary.json).
Every one of the 16 matrices has an integer Gram/residual witness. There are
no positive-definiteness conclusions from floating eigenvalues.

### Why these trees cover every actual local passage

1. A D corner is unpinned and keeps two bonds. With k present internal bonds
   it has at most 2-k actual external factors. Fill only those possible slots,
   using zero fields for missing factors. This is the first statement change.
   U corners still have two arbitrary slots. The local-passage proof applies
   because D sites never carry original pins; the twelve exceptional U
   departures in the second trace retain the paper's existing exceptional bound.
2. Integrate the arrival site. If its two present bonds join the lower corners,
   its contribution is `1+(nL.nR)/5`. This has the positive representation
   `E_xi[(1+sqrt(3/5) xi.nL)(1+sqrt(3/5) xi.nR)]` for a U spin xi.
   Condition on xi. A lower U corner then has at most three fields, so its
   mean has norm at most 3/5 by the existing three-factor lemma. A lower D
   corner has at most one remaining field and hence mean norm at most 1/5.
   They become independent fields at E and W on each conditional fibre.
3. When only one of E,W survives, the other lower U corner has mean at most
   1/2, the intervening D arrival has mean at most 1/10, and the attached U
   corner has mean at most `rho_U(1,1,1/10)=18/35`. If arrival instead retains
   one lower corner and its tip, its effective field has norm at most 3/25;
   the attached U corner is bounded by `rho_U(1,1,3/25)=137/265`.
   Without that field a U leaf has radius 1/2. D leaves have radius 1/5.
4. One case must preserve a correlation: when N is absent, E and W survive,
   both lower corners are U, and arrival joins them, keep the five-vertex lower
   path `E--corner--D--corner--W`. Cutting it into two independent 3/5 fields
   is too weak. Its central sectors have size 36. If N survives, cutting the
   lower connection leaves the five-vertex upper path, whose largest sector
   has size 66. Thus the full eight-site cycle is never a primitive input.
5. A top D corner with two internal bonds has no external factor, and its
   kernel is the positive combination `(3/5)(1+na.nb/3)+2/5`. A top D leaf
   gives one field of radius 1/5; a top U leaf gives radius 1/2. Remaining D
   passage neighbours can be expanded as `(3/5) U + (2/5) delta_0` without
   changing either external configuration. Carry out zero-spin deletions before
   enlarging missing U slots; an unpinned, one-ended corner left by a deleted
   neighbour gives the constant one. Slots are never refilled after that deletion.
   Keep a D neighbour attached only
   to its covering tip as D; its mean radius is exactly at most 3/25.
   Expanding that last D law into a stronger U term is an avoidable loss.
6. Integrate isolated marked vertices to mean balls; add zero fields to compare
   field counts; identify isomorphic paths preserving the outgoing mark and
   the marked-neighbour set; enlarge only componentwise field radii. This
   gives 20 geometries and 38 maximal radius boxes. All scalar normalizers
   removed in these operations are common, positive factors. All convex
   decompositions have the same nonnegative weights for current and next.
   Therefore positivity on every listed model implies positivity before
   the reductions, pointwise in the original pins and outside spins.

The finite geometry audit was independently regenerated from the kept-set

184,914 marked choices, 123,339 N/E choices and 7,962 reflection classes.

### The small mean and scalar inputs

For radii r1,r2,r3, pad with zeros and put

`R_U = ((r1+r2+r3)/3+r1*r2*r3/5)/(1+(r1*r2+r1*r3+r2*r3)/3)`,

`R_D = ((r1+r2+r3)/3+r1*r2*r3/5)/(5/3+(r1*r2+r1*r3+r2*r3)/3)`.

Only 21 triples are needed by the selected family. They are not an assumed
general unequal-radius mean theorem. Their finite proofs reconstruct the
polynomial `25 R^2 (d+x+y+z)^2 - |P|^2`, where d=3 for U and 5 for D,
x=a.b, y=a.c, z=b.c and
`P=(5+z)a+(5+y)b+(5+x)c`. The mean is P/[5(d+x+y+z)]. Substitute
`x=r1*r2*(1-2s)`, and similarly for y,z. All 27 degree-(2,2,2) Bernstein
coefficients are nonnegative, for each of the 21 triples. These are **567
exact rational comparisons, at most 53 bits per numerator/denominator**.
The bound holds on the whole cube, hence on actual Gram data. Positive
normalizers and affinity extend it to the closed balls.

The two special means with radii `(1,1,1/10)` and `(1,1,3/25)` are checked
by the following elementary reduction. With b,c unit, let
`t=|b+c|/2`, `d=2+2t^2`, and alpha be the component of the third field along
b+c. For third-field radius r,

`|15 N|^2 <= (2d alpha+10t)^2 + (d+2)^2 (r^2-alpha^2)`,

`3 Z=d+2t alpha`.

At `R=(5+4r)/(10+5r)`, the difference
`25 R^2(d+2t alpha)^2 - [(2d alpha+10t)^2+(d+2)^2(r^2-alpha^2)]`
has nonnegative Bernstein coefficients on `0<=t<=1`, `-r<=alpha<=r`.
The exact degree-four checks have at most 27-bit coefficients. See
[mean_unit_pair.json](data/mean_unit_pair.json). The bound follows by
orthogonally decomposing b+c and b-c; no numerical optimization is used.

For each independent mean triple `(ro,rf,rg)`, put
`k=rf*rg*(1/3-c/5)`, `L(t)=1-c+k*t` and
`Q(t)=L(t)^2-(ro^2/9)(rf^2+rg^2+2rf*rg*t)`.
The 22 exact tests check L at -1,1 and Q at -1,1 and its vertex when inside
that interval. They imply `L(t)>=0` and `Q(t)>=0`, hence
`Zcur-c Znext>=0`. If the outgoing mark is absent, use ro=0. These scalar
checks also avoid spurious losses from a Pauli lower bound on three
independent mean vectors.

**H3-line finite statement.** Supply four positive vectors z_sigma on
phase x Q, for sigma=+x,-x,+y,-y. For a free edge of displacement d and
coefficient w, use the tilted coefficient `w*r^(sigma.d)`. Check every
ceiling-weighted free row, with a positive integer residual eta. Require
C,D components to be at least V. Supply two integer line arrays
`ell_axis(phase,q,k)`, for `-70<=k<=70`, divided by V. Extend each by

`cap_axis(phase,q,k)=min(z_plus(phase,q)*r^k, z_minus(phase,q)*r^(-k))/V`

outside the interval. Check boundary values against exact cap floors and
use exact cap ceilings for outgoing rows. At k=0 set C,D to one; off k=0
require `0<=ell_C,ell_D<=1`. Check the free one-coordinate Bellman rows
for A,B at every k, and for C,D at k!=0. Beyond radius 70, the tilted-vector
inequalities prove every row analytically. The exponential tail C,D values

`83704870*12^71 <= 2^20*13^71` bounds even the largest decaying branch. See
[line_tail_cap.json](data/line_tail_cap.json).

Define globally

`F_infinity(phase,q,x)=min(ell_x(phase,q,x1-v1), ell_y(phase,q,x2-v2))`.

Positivity gives `T(min(f,g))<=min(Tf,Tg)`. Away from v, an A/B minimizing

has value at most one. Choose that other line. Therefore
`W F_infinity <= F_infinity` off v, and `F_infinity>=g` at v.

### Optional removal of the separate H2 input

The positive tilted-vector margins already imply an open-trail contraction.
For the A phase, the exact data have `Zmin=10486`, `Zmax=83704870` and
`eta/(D*Zmax)>.000125`. Therefore both opposite x-tilted diagonal operators
obey `W_plus Z_plus <= q Z_plus` and `W_minus Z_minus <= q Z_minus`, with
`q=7999/8000`. Put `v_q=sqrt(Z_plus,q * Z_minus,q)`. Cauchy-Schwarz gives

`sum_d w_d v_dest <= sqrt((sum_d w_d r^dx Z_plus,dest)*(sum_d w_d r^(-dx) Z_minus,dest)) <= q v_q`.

The memory-ten open-trail lemma now gives H2. The ratio vmax/vmin is less
than 8000 and
`8000*(200/(187*q))^10 < 16000`, so CV=16000 is sufficient, including
prefixes shorter than ten. These are exact rational comparisons in
[folded_h2.json](data/folded_h2.json). Keeping the original tiny H2 input
instead gives its better q,CV and avoids this additional proof change.
Neither choice materially affects finite checking cost or the final theorem.
The explicit closing calculation remains strict: with retained H2, epsilon
is about 7.65e-15 and A about 1.04e20; with folded H2, epsilon is about
1.12e-19 and A about 3.25e27. Exact rational checks and unrounded epsilon
fractions are in [closing_constants.json](data/closing_constants.json).

## Modified closing argument

actual nonexceptional U departure, every conditional reduced summand has
`Zcur>=c_type Znext`. Undo its common positive factors and sum over outside
spins to obtain `Z_E^tau>=c_type Z_Eplus^tau`, hence
`rho_j^tau<=1/c_type`. D departures still have ratio one and trace weight
1/5. The one-point lemma and the twelve-exception argument are unchanged.

In section 7, retain the exact logarithmic-mean coefficient table. Its
hypotheses use the same cs,ct, so no coefficient recomputation is needed.
Use h=(1,6/5) in the prefix order key and in the straight-before-turn tail
order. The new finite input proves `M1 h<=rho0 h` with rho0=.967725.
Use either the original H2 constants or the folded constants above. With
`C=bar_alpha+2 bar_beta`, `H=max(h)/min(h)=6/5`, set

`b=(1+q)/2`, `r0=q/b`, `B*=2H C CV b^(-2) r0/(1-r0)^3`,

`eps=min((1-b)/2,(1-rho0)/(2B*))`, `theta=1-eps`, `rho=(1+rho0)/2`.

Then `q^2<theta<1`, `rho<1`, and the existing summability calculation gives
`((Mtheta-M1)h)_X/h_X <= eps B* <= (1-rho0)/2`.
Consequently `Mtheta h<=rho h`. The recursion is exactly

`F_X(d)<=sum_{Y,s} M_XY,s F_Y(d-s)+CT*q^(2d)`,

`CT=2(12/5)^24*(CV/(1-q))^2`.

Renewal yields `F_X(d)<=A*theta^d` with
`A=H*max(2,CT)/(1-rho)`. All quantities are finite and positive; no useful
numerical magnitude is required. Sections 5 and 4 then choose the cut,
minorization and one sufficiently large square exactly as before. Thus the
headline existential spectral-gap theorem is unchanged.

In section 8, replace the 7962-pattern operator theorem by H1-tree plus its
structural reduction, and replace the five radius-70 spatial arrays by the

new line-to-plane and patch-to-global lemmas above. Appendix D's Pauli

The boundary-only capacity statement drops the unnecessary exterior terminal
lower bounds and requires U<=Z only on the patch boundary. Its exterior
vector satisfies WZ<=Z; interior target values remain exact. Joint iteration
of Z=max(WZ,boundary U) and the patch Bellman operator gives the tabled
valid, noncontracting bounds. Thus this straightforward relaxation does not
supply the missing factor at R=6 or 8.

The radial search used positive diagonal resolvent gauges, two Poisson
correctors per phase (centered to remove their additive constant), and
profiles `a_p v_p(q)/(s+|x-v+chi_p(q)|^2)^k_p`. Corrector norms are below 3.60;
the diagonal phase growth values are .9795327864, .9405996164, .9405996164,
and .9265148982. Powers (1,1,2,2) and several half-power choices and smoothing
scales 1 through 1024 were tested. At s=1024,R=8 the sampled free rows could
be made contracting, but the needed A amplitude was 2.71 times the fitted
boundary amplitude. The ensuing patch failed the C/D boundary tests quoted
above. Sampling these radial tails was screening, not an exact global proof;
no radial certificate is claimed. Raw and centered searches, fitted
amplitudes, parameters and failure logs are retained under data/logs.

An exact obstruction strengthens the memory-four screening result. A positive
41-bit integer vector on all 108 windows satisfies
`A4 v >= (33854565862584625/33126722421158808) v > 1.02197148 v`,
even at the optimistic constants cs=.963,ct=.936. Thus there is no positive
u with A4 u<=q u, q<1, for that one-step matrix: take a coordinate maximizing
v/u to get a contradiction. This also excludes weaker constants within the
same local-pin and one-step-memory scheme. It does not exclude block extensions
or a changed state/potential. The exact rows, argument and vector are in
[h2_m4_obstruction.json](data/h2_m4_obstruction.json) and
[h2_m4_obstruction_vector.json](data/h2_m4_obstruction_vector.json).

The weighted-lumping experiment used a positive resolvent gauge of the full
memory-ten A matrix (growth .9795327864), projected it to shorter memories,
and bounded each transformed direction coefficient by its class maximum.
For five tested gauge shifts, the best resulting growth bounds were:

Thus this concrete positivity-based coarsening loses too much correlation

## Kernel cost, levels and risk

**Measured finite arithmetic is not measured full Lean time.** Existing costs
115 CPU-hours and 20 CPU-hours are the brief's baselines. Existing production
H1 stage forecasts are 79.64 h constructors, 8.51 h Gram and 15.30 h local
parameters. Applying the measured 327-512x reductions gives a provisional
H1 range **10-35 minutes of Lean CPU**, depending on construction/sharing and
how the new structural coverage lemma is proved. Sixteen modules also remove
most per-pattern import overhead. Literal data are well below 10 MB, even
with straightforward packing. This is a credible Level A target, not a
validated <=15-minute result. It is comfortably a Level B design estimate.

A pure-Std, module-header Lean probe of an actual largest 66-dimensional
integer Gram witness has passed with axioms `[propext]`: 47.92 user seconds,
.50 system seconds, 3,063,956 KiB RSS. Source:
[GramPacked66.lean](probes/GramPacked66.lean). This checks its literal Gram
arithmetic; it does not prove matrix reconstruction or H1-tree soundness.
The full set of 16 literal Gram checks passed in fresh processes: **108.07 CPU seconds total**, maximum **3,061,888 KiB RSS**, all with exactly `[propext]`. Receipts: [gram_kernel_receipts.json](data/gram_kernel_receipts.json).
A 14-dimensional structural-list probe passed in .97 CPU seconds with
511,788 KiB RSS. Initial naive array/list probes either timed out or exposed
a module visibility error; they are not acceptance evidence. The module
visibility error was fixed before these successful probes. All successful
probe axioms are within the allowed set. No native_decide was used.

For H3, the new arithmetic row count is 129,887,520, compared with
1,487,421,600 in the original design: **11.45x**, not 100x. At comparable
packed-checker throughput the 20-hour baseline suggests **about 1.75 CPU-hours**;
a realistic unmeasured range is **1-3 CPU-hours** including prefixes, decoding
and sharing. The line arrays add about 3.4% to the patch row count.
At 21 bits per patch value, 24 per line value and 27 per tilted entry,
compact packed literals need at most 2,744,113,284 data bits. Decimal literal
characters are approximately **826 MB**, plus syntax and declarations.
This fits the Level B literal target if values remain compact in the export;
exporting widened 80-bit guard planes as literals would waste that advantage.
It does not fit Level A's ~200 MB target. Native arrays fit easily below
4 GiB, but no full new Lean process/RSS bound has been measured for H3.

Every generated Lean source can be split below 10,000 physical lines and
begin with `module`; the checked probe files already do. This is a packaging
obligation, not a reason to hide certificates outside the submitted source tree.
The Challenge statement can remain the unchanged existential headline theorem.

The combined package therefore reaches **a numerically validated Level B
candidate**, with H1 near Level A. Neither level has been demonstrated by a
clean build and independent replays. A serial Lean replay plus a 5x NanoDa
replay of a ~2-hour certificate can exceed the 5.5-hour shared budget by a
large margin. More build CPUs alone do not fix serial replay time. The safe
Level A/default-profile target remains open.

Ranked by an explicit engineering judgement of success probability times
finite-work reduction (probabilities are estimates, not measured frequencies):

1. **H1-tree:** probability .8 that the new finite semantic/coverage proof and
   constructor can be completed without a new obstruction; measured work
   reduction 327-512x, score about 300. Main risks are bookkeeping of the
   positive arrival cut, correct partial-pin treatment and efficient new
   forest reconstruction. All required numerical inputs already pass.
2. **H3-line:** probability .9 that the small new min/extension lemmas and the
   current integer arrays can be formalized; measured reduction 11.45x,
   score about 10.3. The proof structure uses only positive operators and
   exact rational comparisons. The unresolved risk is production fit, not
   numerical contraction. This should be combined with H1-tree, but is not
   the requested two-order reduction for H3.

The next mathematical target is to reduce H3's position/memory product by
another factor of roughly 8-15 while preserving its .032 contraction margin.
The tested smaller-memory, scaled-tail, unsplit-case and direction-envelope
routes above do not achieve that. No untested idea is presented as a result.

## Reproduction and receipts

Python numerical dependencies are local in pydeps; use
`PYTHONPATH=pydeps OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`. Key commands:

```
python3 programs/h1_universal_final.py
python3 programs/universal_final_cert.py
python3 programs/universal_exact.py
python3 programs/universal_mean_checks.py
python3 programs/independent_enumeration.py
```

The independent audit also reruns the universal reduction on the newly
enumerated patterns and compares the mathematical descriptors, ignoring
archived IDs and coordinate labels. It gives exactly the same 38 entries.

For H3, `programs/tilted_axes_slack.py` generates search vectors at
`a=log(13/12)`, then `prepare_integer_h3.py` reconstructs all exact coefficients,
checks the tilted rows and writes the integer initial/tail data. Compile
`integer_line.c` and run it with the supplied automaton to build/check
`line_integer.bin`. Compile `integer_patch.c` and run the five `(X,d)` cases
with radius 20, then run `exact_line_prefix.py` on each resulting gzip file.
Commands, full stdout and errors are retained in logs. The patch gzip files
retain the legacy nine-word header and a four-vector block for the existing
prefix reader. That block contains line-at-zero values; the exterior witness
is the separate tilted/line data, rather than that legacy block. The new
integer line/patch checks define their validation. The exact integer
files, the quotient and new source scripts are listed with SHA-256 in
[manifest.json](data/manifest.json); hashes are provenance, not proof evidence.
[result_summary.json](data/result_summary.json) contains exact counts and
per-case receipts. The exact mathematical statements and closing inequalities
are specified above, so the measured data do not become assumptions of the
headline theorem once their Lean checkers and semantic lemmas are proved.
