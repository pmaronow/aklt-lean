Remark 1.1 (Scope of the replacement). P.Definition 6.8 fills two external slots at every D corner,
even when that corner has two present internal bonds. An actual D corner cannot have these additional

supply its geometric hypotheses, and the same exceptional bound is retained. No assertion that the
larger covered-pattern inequality is false is made.

Lemma 1.3 (Positive normalizers, affinity, and leaf summation). A product of at most four field or
bond factors at a site has a strictly positive single-site normalizer when the other spins are fixed in the
closed unit ball. The same holds for a finite network with at most four incident factors at every site.

its bond to x gives
                                               A(1 + µ · nx ).                                          (3)

mean has norm at most 1/5; with its sole field of radius 3/5 it has norm at most 3/25. With at most
